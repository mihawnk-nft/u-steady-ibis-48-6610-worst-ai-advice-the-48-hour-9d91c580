Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-steady-ibis-48-6610-worst-ai-advice-the-48-hour-9d91c580) competition **"Worst AI Advice: The 48-Hour Tight Line Open"** — one line of the worst possible advice an AI could give, in 140 characters or fewer.

## The entry

> Great question! To check if your oven is preheated, press your palm flat on the rack — your skin is a surprisingly accurate thermometer.

136 characters. It opens in the confident, tidy voice of a real AI assistant, names a concrete action a reader could plausibly follow and regret, and lands the joke with a deadpan false rationale.

## How it works

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, no network, no input, no secrets) writes that one line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), validating first that it is 1–140 characters, non-whitespace, and a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
